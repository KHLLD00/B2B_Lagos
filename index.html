The place to be, any time you need it to be

B2B started with a simple idea: a diner shouldn't have to choose a time of day. Whether you're in for a 6am coffee before work or a midnight plate after a long night out, the kitchen doesn't close and the welcome doesn't change.

We serve breakfast alongside dinner, pour cocktails next to milkshakes, and keep the doors open around the clock, seven days a week — because the best diners always have.
